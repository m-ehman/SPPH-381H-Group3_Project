## Project and team
Title: Income and Depression Symptom Patterns in NHANES Adults
Group: Red Team - Yoon Park, Humnah Rashid, Jade Lee, Morgan Ehman

## Audience and KT purpose
Our audience is municipal public-health staff preparing an internal health-equity briefing. Our knowledge translation (KT) framing is that the data can point to a pattern between income and depressive symptoms that is worth discussing, while making clear it is not evidence of cause. It is intended to support a discussion about what deserves closer survey-aware analysis, rather than an immediate policy decision.

## Questions
Primary question: How does family income to poverty ratio associated with depression symptoms among U.S. adults?

Secondary question: How does the association differ across age groups?

## Data and planned output
We will use the cached CSV derived from CDC/NCHS NHANES public-use files. The planned outputs are a Table 1-style summary, one family income to poverty ratio-by-depression symptom figure, one age stratified family income to poverty ratio-by-depression symptom figure, and a short Quarto report. 

## Known limitations
The PHQ-9 is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Family income to poverty ratio variable is missing for some participants. The survey is cross-sectional, so it cannot show cause. Unweighted results describe the sample only, not the U.S. population.
