# AI Use Note

AI was used to help interpret the M1 requirements, brainstorm and refine the research questions, and organize and improve the wording and structure across the M1 submission files.

AI was also used to help identify relevant NHANES variables and suggest how the variables could best be represented in the planned analysis and visualization.

The group manually verified dataset descriptions, variable definitions, coding, and other factual claims using the official CDC/NCHS NHANES documentation. We also reviewed the proposed research questions, analysis plan, and visual plan to confirm that they were feasible with the selected NHANES data and that the final wording reflected the group’s own decisions.
