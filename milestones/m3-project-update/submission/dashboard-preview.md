# Dashboard preview

- Current source: `dashboard/dashboard.qmd`
- Rendered preview: `dashboard/dashboard.html`
- Intended audience: staff in a municipal public health department who are preparing an internal briefing on health equity
- Feature: change the single `selected_band` value and re-render to update the table, the figure title and highlighted point, and the interpretation sentence for that income band
- Limitation/privacy caution: The dashboard shows survey-weighted aggregate statistics, not individual records. The results are descriptive and do not establish a causal relationship between income and depression symptoms.
